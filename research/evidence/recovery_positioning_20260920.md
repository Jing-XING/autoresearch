# Recovery manuscript: primary-source positioning check

Checked on 20 September 2026 while revising the incomplete P3 manuscript.
These are literature observations, not additional experimental replications.

- [Verified Tool Calls, v1, sections 4.4, 4.7 and 7](https://arxiv.org/html/2608.02645v1):
  verification before retry, three-valued postcondition outcomes and verifier
  quality are already explicit. P3 cannot claim these ideas as its method.
- [Where Agent Frameworks Fall Short, v4, implications](https://arxiv.org/html/2602.21806v4):
  version-aware API-sequence and state-transition testing already follows
  from the authors' issue analysis. P3 instead supplies selected executable
  component cases with saved native post-states, without a broad taxonomy claim.
- [RAC, v2, evaluation sections 5.3-5.4](https://arxiv.org/html/2605.03409v2):
  the model experiments cover predictable and dynamic failures. P3's constructed
  controls do not reproduce that evaluation or revise its reported scores.

The proposed empirical contribution is a bounded conjunction of error
consumption, declared accounting contracts, matched component controls and
negative archival screens. This positioning remains an argument to be assessed,
not proof of novelty, venue suitability or acceptance. The main manuscript
keeps dependency-version and source-selection limits explicit. No external
reviewer, independent human adjudication or model performance gain is claimed.
