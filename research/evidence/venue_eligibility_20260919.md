# Venue eligibility checks, 19 September 2026

These are internal constraints, not a promised publication schedule.

The [official AAMAS 2027 main-track call](https://warwick.ac.uk/fac/sci/dcs/aamas2027/calls/call-for-main-track/)
lists OpenReview author registration on 17 September, abstract submission
on 1 October, papers on 8 October and notification on 21 December 2026,
all end-of-day UTC-12. The registration date has passed; the user's existing
author-account status is unknown. Do not assume eligibility or register,
contact organizers or submit a manuscript without concrete publication
materials and the required author information. The GAAI area covers agent
evaluation, memory, tools and runtime support, but generic prompting/tool
use without a clear agent contribution is explicitly outside scope.

The [submission instructions](https://warwick.ac.uk/fac/sci/dcs/aamas2027/guidelines-and-policies/instructions/)
require English, double-blind PDF, mandatory LaTeX and at most eight main
pages plus references. The [Findings policy](https://warwick.ac.uk/fac/sci/dcs/aamas2027/guidelines-and-policies/findings/)
automatically considers unselected main-track papers for Findings unless
authors opt out. That category must not be presented as satisfying the
requested CCF conference threshold merely because it has the same length.

The [CCF seventh-edition announcement](https://www.ccf.org.cn/Academic_Evaluation/By_category/)
was published 31 March 2026 and notes an April correction. It limits listed
conference recognition to full/regular papers, excluding short papers,
Findings, demos and workshops. Search-indexed official text was retrieved;
direct page opening returned HTTP 405. The [official AI category page](https://www.ccf.org.cn/Academic_Evaluation/AI/)
lists AAMAS and ICAPS as B, but its displayed IJCAI listing differs from
reported seventh-edition changes. Therefore it is insufficient to establish
that the category table itself has been synchronized to the 2026 PDF.
The current official PDF remains to be verified. Third-party transcriptions
are not substituted for that verification. CAS and JCR quartiles remain
separate unverified criteria.
