## Concrete journal fit

[JAAMAS's publisher page](https://link.springer.com/journal/10458) covers
foundational, theoretical and practical autonomous-agent research. It reports
a nine-day median from submission to first decision on the date checked.
That metric is not an acceptance or peer-review-completion estimate. The
regular-paper route is a plausible fit for an empirical study of tool-agent
evidence and evaluation; this is a scope judgment, not editorial endorsement.

[JAAMAS author instructions](https://link.springer.com/journal/10458/submission-guidelines)
require a one-to-two-page information sheet identifying the main contribution,
specific supporting evidence, closest work and prior publication relationship.
The abstract should be 150–250 words. Mathematical manuscripts may use LaTeX;
editable source is required. Substantive LLM involvement must be documented,
LLMs cannot be authors, and final human accountability remains necessary.
Prepare these materials with the completed manuscript; do not fabricate
author identities, affiliations, funding or human annotation/review.

[Applied Intelligence's publisher page](https://link.springer.com/journal/10489)
reports a 45-day median to first decision. It cannot satisfy the CCF-B route
because the official 2026 entry is C. This check does not establish its JCR
or CAS eligibility, and no final target has been chosen for that journal.

No journal submission, editorial contact, author approval or acceptance has
